# Sheikhy (شيخي) — Marketing Pitch for Instructors

---

## What Is Sheikhy?

**Sheikhy** is a dedicated mobile application (iOS & Android) built exclusively for you — the instructor. Unlike general platforms, each instance of the app carries **your name, your brand color, and your content only**. Your students open the app and see nothing but your teaching.

---

## The Core Problem We Solve

Every Islamic instructor today faces the same fragmented reality:

| Pain Point | How Most Instructors Handle It Today |
|---|---|
| Teaching video content | YouTube — public, full of distractions, competing suggestions |
| Audio lessons | Telegram channels — unstructured, no tracking |
| PDF resources | WhatsApp groups — buried in chat, no organization |
| Student questions | Comments, DMs, Telegram — scattered and hard to manage |
| Assessing students | Google Forms, external tools — disconnected from content |
| Proof of completion | Manually issued, or not issued at all |
| Knowing who's actually learning | No visibility |

**Sheikhy brings all of this into one clean, structured, professional platform — under your name.**

---

## Why Your Students Will Love It

### 1. Zero Distractions — This Is the Real Difference
When a student watches your lesson on YouTube, YouTube's algorithm immediately suggests other videos alongside it. Ads interrupt. Shorts pop up. The student leaves before the lesson ends.

**In Sheikhy, there are no ads. There are no suggested videos. There is no algorithm competing for your student's attention.** The screen shows your lesson and nothing else. The student's only choice is to continue learning.

### 2. Structured Learning Path
Content is organized into **scientific sections** (عقيدة، فقه، تفسير، سيرة...), then books, then sequential lessons. Students always know where they are and what comes next.

### 3. Pick Up Exactly Where They Left Off
The app saves each student's exact video position. With one tap on "Continue Learning," they resume from the exact second they stopped — even weeks later.

### 4. Real Progress Visibility
Every student sees a progress bar on every book they've enrolled in. Completed lessons are checked. They can see, at a glance, how far they've come — which is a proven motivator to keep going.

### 5. Lesson Quizzes for Real Understanding
Each lesson can have a short quiz attached to it. Students test their comprehension immediately after the content, reinforcing what they just learned.

### 6. A Proper Final Exam
At the end of each book, a timed, multiple-choice final exam awaits. You set the passing grade, the number of allowed attempts, and the time limit. The app enforces it automatically.

### 7. Downloadable Certificates
Students who pass the final exam receive a **digitally generated certificate** with their name, the book title, the date, and their score. They can download and share it as a PDF — a real, proud achievement.

### 8. Achievement Tracking
A dedicated screen shows each student their total completed lessons, study hours, completed books, and a GitHub-style activity heatmap for the year. They can see their own dedication reflected back at them.

### 9. Ask the Sheikh — Directly
From inside any lesson, a student can submit a question to you. You answer it from your dashboard. The answer becomes visible to **all students of that lesson** — meaning one answer benefits everyone, not just the asker. The asker's identity is always private from other students.

### 10. Push Notifications
You can send announcements, reminders, or live-stream links directly to every student's phone with a single tap.

## Why You (the Instructor) Will Love It

### 1. Your Own App, Under Your Name
This is not a marketplace where you compete with other instructors. This is **your app**, branded with your name and your color. Students associate the entire experience with you.

### 2. No Technical Complexity
You don't upload videos anywhere new. You paste the YouTube link you already have. You paste the Google Drive link for audio or PDF. The app does the rest. No servers to manage. No encoding. No storage bills.

### 3. Zero Hosting Cost
- **Video:** YouTube (free, unlimited)
- **Audio & PDF:** Google Drive (free)
- **Database & Auth:** Supabase free tier
- **Push Notifications:** Firebase free tier

**The recurring cost to run Sheikhy is effectively zero for any normal teaching volume.**

### 4. Full Student Engagement Visibility
For every book you publish, you see:
- Total enrolled students
- Average exam score (with a visual star rating)
- Enrollment trend over time
- The 2 most recent student questions

You know exactly which books students are engaging with and how they're performing.

### 5. Q&A Inbox — Organized, Not Chaotic
All student questions are organized in a dedicated inbox per book, divided into "Awaiting Reply" and "Replied." You answer inline, in the app, without switching to Telegram or WhatsApp. Each question is linked to the specific lesson it came from.

### 6. Publish on Your Schedule
Every lesson and book has a draft/published toggle. Build your content privately, then publish when you're ready. You control visibility completely.

### 7. Feature the Content That Matters
You can manually select which books appear prominently on the Home screen — the first thing students see when they open the app. You set the order. You control the spotlight.

### 8. Home Banners You Control
A carousel on the Home screen can display announcements, new book launches, or important reminders. You add, edit, and reorder them from your dashboard at any time.

### 9. Organized by Scientific Discipline
Your content is grouped into proper Islamic academic sections — not a flat, undifferentiated list. Students navigate to exactly the area of knowledge they're seeking.

### 10. Sequential Learning with Lesson Locking
Lessons unlock in order. A student cannot jump to lesson 5 without completing lessons 1–4. This ensures the learning path you designed is actually followed.

## How Content Reaches Students (The Simple Flow)

```
You paste a YouTube link        →  Students watch in-app, no distractions
You paste a Google Drive link   →  Students play audio or view PDF in-app
You write quiz questions         →  Students test themselves after each lesson
You write the final exam         →  App automatically grades and issues certificates
You answer a question            →  All students in that lesson see the answer
You send a notification          →  Every student's phone receives it instantly
```

---

## The Learning Experience — End to End

1. Student registers with their email
2. Browses your scientific sections
3. Enrolls in a book (with confirmation — not accidental)
4. Studies each lesson in sequence: watches, listens, reads, quizzes
5. Completes the book
6. Takes the final exam (timed, multiple-choice, auto-graded)
7. Passes → receives a certificate with their name on it
8. Achievements screen reflects their growing journey
